Behind The Red Flags 🚩

The Vibe & Vision

The Red Flag Calculator is an interactive Gen-Z relationship assessment tool featuring over 100+ handcrafted savage questions, Fisher-Yates randomizers, and polynomial hashing algorithms.

The Two Modes

  • Solo Red Flag Quiz: Evaluate your partner, crush, or ex against 10 randomized relationship red flag questions.
  • Duo Toxicity Match Calculator: Take a 10-question dual-assessment test to calculate exact mutual toxicity chemistry scores.

The Developer & Inspiration

Created by Aditya Sharma (aadiii). Inspired by a previous fun love calculator project, this upgraded edition flips classic tropes on their head with high-tier Gen-Z humor and toxic dating culture references.
